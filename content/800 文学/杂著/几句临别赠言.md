几句临别赠言

"没有什么是原创的。“窃取”任何地方的灵感或激发你的想象力的共鸣，助长你的想象力。吞噬旧电影、新电影、音乐、书籍、绘画、照片、诗歌、梦境、随机的谈话、建筑、桥梁和其他东西、

照片、诗歌、梦想、随机对话、建筑、桥梁，街头标志、树木、云彩、水体、光线和阴影。

只选择那些直接与你的灵魂对话的东西来“偷窃”如果你这样做，你的作品（和“偷窃”）将是真实的。真实性是无价的；“原创性”是不存在的。而且不要费力地掩盖你的“偷窃行为”--如果你想的话，就庆祝一下吧。

在任何情况下，永远记住让-吕克-戈达尔的话：

不是你从哪里拿东西，而是你把它们带到哪里。

A Few Parting Words

"**Nothing is original.** Steal from anywhere that resonates with inspiration or fuels your imagination. Devour old films, new films, music, books, paintings, photographs, poems, dreams, random conversations, architecture, bridges, street signs, trees, clouds, bodies of water, light and shadows.

Select only things to steal from that speak directly to your soul. If you do this, your work (and theft) will be authentic. Authenticity is invaluable; originality is non-existent. And don't bother concealing your thievery - celebrate it if you feel like it. In any case, always remember what Jean-Luc Godard said:

**'It's not where you take things from - it's where you take them to."'**

James Robert Jarmusch

American film director, screenwriter, actor, producer, editor, and compose
